solve_stationary_distribution

ludics.solve_stationary_distribution(transition_matrix, residual_tolerance=10**-8,
density_threshold=0.25)

Solves for the unique stationary distribution of a transition matrix, or returns None when the chain does not have exactly one.

Writing \(\pi T = \pi\) as \((T^{\mathsf{T}} - I)\pi^{\mathsf{T}} = 0\) and replacing the last row with the condition that the entries sum to one gives a linear system whose solution is the stationary distribution. The system is factorised sparsely when few of its entries are non zero and densely when most of them are.

A chain with two or more absorbing states has a family of stationary distributions rather than one, which makes the system singular. There is then no single answer to return, so None is returned and the caller may iterate instead. This is what compute_steady_state does.

Parameters:

  • transition_matrix: numpy.array or scipy.sparse array - a transition matrix. A sparse array is read without being converted
  • residual_tolerance: float - how far \(\pi T\) may sit from \(\pi\) for the solution to be accepted
  • density_threshold: float - the proportion of non zero entries at which the system stops being factorised sparsely and is factorised densely instead

Returns:

  • numpy.array or None - the stationary distribution, or None when the chain does not have exactly one
